# COVID-19 Corporate Credit Risk Analysis
An analysis of the impact of COVID-19 on the financial health and credit risk of 10 major U.S. companies from 2019–2022.

This project will show how the COVID-19 pandemic affected 10 major companies (across different industries) in the US from a financial health and credit risk standpoint.

There will be usage of financial statement data from 2019-2022, the analysis will evaluate the changes in liquidity, leverage, profitability, and debt-servicing capacity. 

# Research Question
How did COVID-19 affect the financial health and credit risk of the 10 major U.S. companies, and which companies recovered most strongly?

# Research Objectives
- Evaluate the company's financial performance, before, during, and after the initial COVID-19 shock.
- Compare liquidity and leverage across the companies.
- Assess debt-servicing capcity and operating cash flow.
- Develop an analytical credit risk socring framework.
- Identify companies that demonstrated the strongest financial recovery.

# Companies and Industries
Airlines: Delta Air Lines (DAL), American Airlines (AAL)
Retail: Walmart (WMT), Target (TGT)
Technology: NVIDVIA (NVDA), Microsoft (MSFT)
Consumer Goods: Procter & Gamble (PG), Coca-Cola (KO)
Banking: JPMorgan Chase (JPM), Bank of America (BAC)

# Analysis Period
2019: Pre-pandemic baseline
2020: Initial pandemic shock
2021: Recovery period
2022: Post-shock assessment

# Key Financial Metrics
- Current Ratio
- Debt-to-Equity Ratio
- Interest Coverage Ratio
- Operating Cash Flow to Total Debt
- Debt-to-EBITDA
- Revenue Growth
- Operating Profit Margin

# Tools and Technologies
Python, R, RStudio, Github, Financial statement analysis, Data visualization
